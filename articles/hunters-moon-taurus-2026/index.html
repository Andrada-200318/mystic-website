The 2026 Hunter’s Moon in Taurus: Grounding your Deepest Desires

How October’s powerful lunar event will challenge your self-worth, rewrite your relationship with abundance, and test the zodiac.

By Ramona Sîrbu · 25 October 2026

✦

On October 25-26, 2026, the sky will host the Hunter’s Full Moon in the earthy, sensual sign of Taurus. Because the Moon directly opposes the Sun in intense, transformative Scorpio, this lunation creates a cosmic tug-of-war between our desire for safety and the inevitable need for change. Taurus rules security, possessions, and personal values, making this the perfect moment to examine what is truly worth holding onto as the year tilts toward winter.

The Major Astrological Aspects

  • The Taurus-Scorpio Axis: The Moon in Taurus seeks stability, comfort, and physical pleasure, while the Sun in Scorpio demands emotional depth, vulnerability, and transformation. This opposition highlights the balance between what we possess physically and what we share intimately.
  • Venus at Anaretic Degree: Venus, the planetary ruler of Taurus, sits at 29 degrees Libra, the highly charged, karmic final degree of the sign. This placement signals a climax or breaking point in matters of relationships, diplomacy, and financial partnerships.
  • Mars in Leo Adds Fire: Mars is hovering at 15 degrees Leo, introducing a bold, performative, and potentially combative energy. This squares the Moon and Sun, creating friction that could cause simple pleasure-seeking to morph into recklessness and financial trouble if left unchecked.

The Most Influenced Zodiac Signs

This Full Moon heavily targets the Fixed signs, challenging their notorious resistance to change.

  • Taurus (Sun, Moon, or Rising): This lunation occurs in your home sign, shining a massive spotlight on your identity and self-worth. You are being pushed to examine your relationship with money, possessions, and physical abundance. Limiting beliefs about what you deserve are surfacing so they can be released. Be heavily mindful of overindulgence or retail therapy as a coping mechanism for stress.
  • Scorpio (Sun, Moon, or Rising): With the Sun in your sign and the Moon in your opposite sector, your closest relationships are under the microscope. The tension between your intense emotional boundaries and a partner’s need for simplicity and stability will reach a boiling point. It is a time to compromise without sacrificing your core values.
  • Leo (Sun, Moon, or Rising): Mars is burning through your sign, giving you a surge of ambition and a desire to take the lead. However, this fiery energy directly clashes with the slow, deliberate pace of the Taurus Moon. You may feel deeply frustrated by delays in your career or creative projects. Channel this excess energy into a physical outlet rather than forcing a situation before it is ready.
  • Aquarius (Sun, Moon, or Rising): You will feel the tension of this Moon in the foundational areas of your life—home, family, and long-term security. You are being asked to balance your desire for rebellious individuality with the very real need for a stable, comfortable sanctuary to return to at the end of the day.

How to Harness the Energy

The Taurus Full Moon asks you to slow down and get grounded. Keep things simple: write down one limiting belief about your finances or self-worth that you want to leave behind. Focus on steady progress rather than dramatic shifts, and remember that accepting change is ultimately the key to long-term security.
