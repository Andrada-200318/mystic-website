From Garnet to Turquoise: The Twelve Birthstones of the Year

What the stone of your birth month is said to bring you, from protection and passion to peace of mind.

By Andrada Alexiuc · 1 November 2026

✦

Every sign of the zodiac has its birthstones, yet look for them online and you will find many different charts. There is good reason for this: as new stones were discovered throughout history, they were assigned to the signs in turn, and different cultures have long followed systems of their own.

January Garnet February Amethyst March Aquamarine April Diamond May Emerald June Pearl July Ruby August Peridot September Sapphire October Opal November Topaz December Turquoise

You may also notice that many charts give each sign a main stone along with alternatives. The stones first chosen were often rare and very expensive, yet people still longed to wear them for protection, and so alternatives were offered that work around the same idea for each sign. Here are the twelve stones of the year, month by month.

January: Garnet

Garnet is a jewel of deep winter, the stone of those born under Capricorn and Aquarius. It stands for awareness, frankness, consideration and a peace-loving heart, and it is said to grant its wearer the power to protect and defend what they love.

Old lore kept it close as a ward against nightmares and snakebites, and as a guiding light through the dark. Known as the gemstone of passion, garnet is said to kindle desire and keep harmony between lovers, which is why it is also given as the second-anniversary stone, to preserve the love a couple has built.

February: Amethyst

Amethyst is a semi-precious stone with deep ties to royalty and the noble world. It is said to bring power and ambition and to clear the mind of the one who wears it, which is why it is often worn before meditation for its purifying touch.

Its name comes from the ancient Greek for “not drunken”, for it was trusted to clear the head of those left dizzy by alcohol. Fine amethysts have been set in religious jewellery and royal crown jewels for ages, and the stone was once valued as highly as ruby, emerald and sapphire. Closer to home, amethyst is kept for easing stress, stirring inspiration and inviting good sleep, and for filling a room with a peace so gentle that arguments find no place to begin.

March: Aquamarine

Aquamarine carries the calm of still water. It is said to bring serenity and rest, and to watch protectively over the water sign of Pisces. Those who keep it close look to it for emotional clarity, for staying present with their feelings and for voicing them without hesitation, which makes it a stone of open, honest conversation.

It is also believed to strengthen our connection with ourselves and with others, to bring emotional balance, to cool a quick temper or an impulsive streak, and to foster harmony between partners. In meditation, aquamarine is used to deepen intuition, to follow the flow of emotion and to awaken spiritual awareness.

April: Diamond

Diamond is the very picture of luxury, the essence of beauty held in a stone that also stands for strength and durability. Formed under immense heat and pressure deep within the Earth, it has long been tied to brilliance, hardness and timeless appeal. It is the stone of those born under Aries and Taurus.

Diamonds are also thought to hold supernatural powers, and many have trusted them as shields against panic, evil spells and enchantments. Above all, a diamond is said to amplify whatever surrounds it: steeped in positive energy, it evokes love, care and passion, while in the presence of negativity, it may become cursed. Lore also credits it with the power to heal sleepwalkers and to forge a lasting flame between partners.

May: Emerald

Emerald is the stone of those born under Taurus and Gemini, and it is deeply tied to the goddess Venus. Since the dawn of civilisation, these green stones have been wrapped in spiritual meaning, and through that bond, emerald is said to evoke passion, joy and unconditional love. It is believed to amplify love itself, deepening the feelings between two people so that their bond may grow for ever. It is also linked with fertility, new beginnings, vitality and youth.

When it comes to supernatural gifts, emerald is a stone of vision: the power to see into the future through intuition and foretelling. For this reason, it has long been prized as a stone of wisdom and great strength of mind.

June: Pearl

Formed in the depths of the ocean, pearls have been linked since time immemorial with purity, humility and innocence. They may seem simple and modest at first glance, yet they carry a wealth of gifts inherited from the Mother Sea. Pearls are believed to prolong life and to hold prosperity for those who own them.

In Greek myth, pearls were tears of happiness shed by Aphrodite, the goddess of love, and so they are said to bring joy, unity and strength to the couples who share them. They are also considered gems of wisdom, luck and success, and they belong to those born under Gemini and Cancer.

July: Ruby

Ruby is the guardian stone of those born under Cancer and Leo. In ancient times it was known as the king of precious stones for its rarity, hardness and beauty. Of the well-known gems, only diamond is harder, and a trace of chromium gives ruby both its red and a glow that seems to burn from within. It was also held to have mystical powers: its blood-red colour made it a keeper of the power of life and a symbol of youthful energy.

Ruby is said to raise energy levels, to turn the mind towards more positive thoughts and to inspire self-assurance, while stirring desire and sensual energy. It is also worn as a stone of protection, believed to repel the negative energy around you, to warm the heart and to encourage self-love.

August: Peridot

Peridot belongs to Leo and Virgo, the two signs that share the month of August. It is above all a stone of renewal, prosperity and emotional balance, and it is thought to carry a protective energy, which is why people keep it as a talisman against negativity. Its green glow also ties it to healing and the vitality of the natural world.

Peridot is said to bring self-awareness, letting you take an honest look at yourself and see how you might change for the better. It is believed to soften the way you see yourself, easing feelings of guilt and remorse and loosening obsessive habits, and to make it easier to let go of bottled-up feelings such as anger and jealousy.

September: Sapphire

The September birthstone has traditionally stood for sincerity, truth, faithfulness and nobility, and it belongs to Virgo and Libra, the two signs that share the month. We picture sapphire in deep blue, yet it is the same mineral as July’s ruby: both are corundum, and only their colour tells them apart.

That blue has always tied it to the heavens. Sapphire was thought to attract divine favour and wisdom, and it remains a stone of harmony and prosperity. The elite of ancient Greece and Rome wore it to ward off harm and envy, and medieval bishops set it in their rings as an emblem of heaven.

Common belief holds that sapphire can guide your thoughts, protect you on your journeys and calm a troubled heart.

October: Opal

Opal belongs to Libra and Scorpio, the two signs that share the month of October, and few stones are as enchanting to behold. Deep within it, microscopic spheres of silica split the light, so that every colour of the rainbow seems to dance across its surface.

For most of history, opal was regarded as the luckiest and most magical of all gems, because it can show every colour at once. Arabic legend tells that it fell from the heavens in flashes of lightning, while the ancient Greeks believed it gave its owner the gift of prophecy and guarded them from disease. Across Europe, it has long been a symbol of hope, purity and truth.

November: Topaz

Topaz belongs to Scorpio and Sagittarius, the two signs that share the month of November. It is above all a stone of warmth, joy and abundance, said to bring a sunlit clarity to the mind and to help you see your goals and your own worth more truly. Those who work with it often turn to it when setting intentions, trusting it to draw good fortune and to give their wishes the strength to take root.

Topaz is also thought to be a stone of inner strength. It is said to break spells and dispel anger, to lift heavy moods and to encourage truth and forgiveness, leaving room for confidence and generosity to grow.

December: Turquoise

Turquoise belongs to Sagittarius and Capricorn, the two signs that share the month of December. Holding the colours of both sky and water, it is seen as a bridge between heaven and earth, a stone of protection, wholeness and inner peace. It is said to shield the one who wears it from negative energy and ill will, and travellers carry it as a companion for safe journeys.

Turquoise is also linked with honest speech, believed to help you find the right words and speak your truth with kindness. Those who keep it close look to it for emotional balance and deeper friendships, and in meditation it is used to calm the mind, strengthen intuition and awaken spiritual awareness.
