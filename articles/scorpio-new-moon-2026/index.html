The Scorpio New Moon: Unlocking the Secrets of November 9, 2026

How this powerful lunar event will challenge your grudges and rewrite your emotional reality.

By Ramona Sîrbu · 7 November 2026

✦

If you have been holding onto old grudges, nursing bruised pride, or hiding from some uncomfortable emotional truths, the cosmos is about to stage an intervention. On Monday, November 9th, 2026, the New Moon arrives in the deep, fixed water sign of Scorpio.

While New Moons generally symbolize fresh starts, Scorpio is not interested in superficial changes or simply slapping a new coat of paint over a crumbling foundation. This lunation is about getting to the absolute bottom of things, facing the problems you’ve swept under the rug, and doing something meaningful about them.

Adding to the intensity, this New Moon coincides with both Mercury and Venus being in retrograde. Astrologers traditionally view this combination as a mandate to slow down and rethink past decisions, particularly concerning love, money, and personal values. It is a time for reflection, repair, and reorganization rather than rushing into dramatic grand openings.

What Does It Mean for Your Element?

Water Signs

Cancer · Scorpio · Pisces

You will feel this intensity the most. Heightened intuition will guide you through deep emotional processing. Embrace these feelings, as they will cleanse and renew your spirit. For Scorpio specifically, this New Moon in your sign offers the gift of a personal rebrand, a gentler, more accurate vision of who you are now.

Earth Signs

Taurus · Virgo · Capricorn

You are being called to ground your emotional insights into tangible changes. For Taurus, this means evaluating whether your romantic and professional commitments offer mutual respect and support. For Capricorn, the focus shifts to reconnecting with friends and professional allies who share your long-term vision.

Fire Signs

Aries · Leo · Sagittarius

This Moon ignites your courage to confront hidden truths. Leo will find the energy to prioritize home, family, and emotional security, showing grace to those around them. Aries should focus on financial stability and releasing emotional fears in close relationships.

Air Signs

Gemini · Libra · Aquarius

Your mental agility is needed to process complex emotions. Libra should review finances and spending habits, looking for practical ways to secure long-term stability. Aquarius is urged to review career plans and ambitions, identifying the skills needed for their next milestone.

How to Work with the Energy

Astrologers advise against launching major new projects or signing binding contracts right now. Instead, focus on the “re” words: reconnect, revisit, repair, release, and renew.

To harness this energy, take ten quiet minutes between Sunday night and Wednesday. Write down two lists: “What I Am Done Carrying” and “What I Am Ready to Receive”, keeping the focus on Scorpio themes like trust, intimacy, money, and self-worth. If you practice manifestation, wait a day or two after the New Moon before setting your intentions.
