What Are the Zodiac Signs?

A guide to the twelve signs of the zodiac, their elements, their modalities and the traits traditionally linked to each one.

By Ștefan Murariu · 27 October 2026

✦

Astrology is a belief system that is not scientifically supported. It explores the possible connections between the positions of the Sun, Moon, and planets at the time of a person’s birth and their personality traits, emotions, relationships, and life experiences.

In astrology, the 12 zodiac signs are divided into four groups based on the traditional elements: earth, air, fire, and water. Each sign is associated with specific personality traits and characteristics.

What Are the Three Zodiac Modalities and What Do They Mean?

In astrology, the three modalities describe how each zodiac sign is traditionally believed to approach action, stability, and change.

  • Cardinal signs: People born under cardinal signs are considered natural initiators who enjoy starting new projects, taking action, and creating change.
  • Fixed signs: People born under fixed signs are considered determined, reliable, and persistent. They tend to value stability and are often associated with maintaining what they have established.
  • Mutable signs: People born under mutable signs are considered adaptable, flexible, and open to change. They are often associated with adjusting to new situations and finding different ways to approach challenges.

The Twelve Zodiac Signs

♈︎

Aries

The first zodiac sign is Aries. The Aries zodiac sign is associated with people born between March 21 and April 19. It belongs to the fire element and has a cardinal modality. Aries people are considered bold, energetic, and adventurous.

♉︎

Taurus

The second zodiac sign is Taurus. The Taurus zodiac sign is associated with people born between April 20 and May 20. It belongs to the earth element and has a fixed modality. Taurus people are considered reliable, practical, and sometimes stubborn.

♊︎

Gemini

The third zodiac sign is Gemini. The Gemini zodiac sign is associated with people born between May 21 and June 20. It belongs to the air element and has a mutable modality. Gemini people are considered curious, quick-witted, and inconsistent.

♋︎

Cancer

The fourth zodiac sign is Cancer. The Cancer zodiac sign is associated with people born between June 21 and July 22. It belongs to the water element and has a cardinal modality. Cancer people are considered loyal, protective, and a little moody.

♌︎

Leo

The fifth zodiac sign is Leo. The Leo zodiac sign is associated with people born between July 23 and August 22. It belongs to the fire element and has a fixed modality. Leo people are considered creative, generous, and domineering.

♍︎

Virgo

The sixth zodiac sign is Virgo. The Virgo zodiac sign is associated with people born between August 23 and September 22. It belongs to the earth element and has a mutable modality. Virgo people are considered analytical, critical, and hardworking.

♎︎

Libra

The seventh zodiac sign is Libra. The Libra zodiac sign is associated with people born between September 23 and October 22. It belongs to the air element and has a cardinal modality. Libra people are considered diplomatic, cooperative, and indecisive.

♏︎

Scorpio

The eighth zodiac sign is Scorpio. The Scorpio zodiac sign is associated with people born between October 23 and November 21. It belongs to the water element and has a fixed modality. Scorpio people are considered passionate, resourceful, and sometimes jealous.

♐︎

Sagittarius

The ninth zodiac sign is Sagittarius. The Sagittarius zodiac sign is associated with people born between November 22 and December 21. It belongs to the fire element and has a mutable modality. Sagittarius people are considered adventurous, honest, and tactless.

♑︎

Capricorn

The tenth zodiac sign is Capricorn. The Capricorn zodiac sign is associated with people born between December 22 and January 19. It belongs to the earth element and has a cardinal modality. Capricorn people are considered disciplined, responsible, and sometimes unforgiving.

♒︎

Aquarius

The eleventh zodiac sign is Aquarius. The Aquarius zodiac sign is associated with people born between January 20 and February 18. It belongs to the air element and has a fixed modality. Aquarius people are considered independent, intellectual, and detached.

♓︎

Pisces

The twelfth zodiac sign is Pisces. The Pisces zodiac sign is associated with people born between February 19 and March 20. It belongs to the water element and has a mutable modality. Pisces people are considered artistic, compassionate, and escapist.
