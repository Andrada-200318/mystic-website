Tea Leaf Reading

The old art of tasseography reads the shapes that loose leaves leave behind. Hold a question in your mind as you drink, swirl what is left three times, and turn the cup over onto its saucer. Then see what the leaves have to say.

  1. Sip
  2. Swirl
  3. Turn
  4. Read
