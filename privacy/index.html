Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Mystic Space is a small website of tarot, moon and wish rituals. We do not ask for your name, email address or any account. This page explains the little we do collect and why.

Visitor statistics

We use two tools to count visits, so we can see which pages people enjoy:

  • Google Analytics records which pages you visit, roughly where you are in the world (country or city), what kind of device and browser you use, and how you found us. It stores cookies on your device to recognise return visits, so it only runs if you choose Accept in our cookie banner. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy.
  • GoatCounter counts page views without cookies and without tracking you from one visit to the next.

What stays on your device

Some rituals remember things for you: the Moon Altar remembers your last ritual, the Star Weaver keeps the constellations you have drawn, the Wish Globe counts how many wishes you made today, and the site remembers your answer to the cookie banner. This is saved only in your own browser and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser's data for this site removes it.

Birth charts and the Chinese zodiac

Your birth chart and Chinese zodiac sign are calculated entirely in your browser. Your birth date and time are never sent to us or anyone else. To find your birthplace on the map, the place name you type is sent to Open-Meteo, a free geocoding service, which returns its coordinates and time zone. Open-Meteo describes how it handles requests in its privacy policy.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Your choices

You can change your answer at any time with the link, also found at the bottom of our home page. If you decline after accepting, we stop Google Analytics and remove its cookies. You can also block cookies in your browser settings. The rituals work the same either way.

Questions

If you have a question about this policy, or would like us to remove any data, please get in touch.
